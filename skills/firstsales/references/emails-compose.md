# Direct email and compose

Direct email sends one email to one contact outside a campaign. Every send, schedule and approve reaches a real person: show subject, body, sender and recipient, and get a yes first.

```bash
firstsales emails draft --contact contact_123 --connector conn_123 --subject "Quick question" --body "Hi Jane" --idempotency-key draft-001 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales emails send --contact contact_123 --connector conn_123 --subject "Quick question" --body "Hi Jane" --idempotency-key send-001 --dry-run --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales emails schedule --contact contact_123 --connector conn_123 --subject "Follow-up" --body "Hi Jane" --at 2026-11-01T10:00:00+05:30 --idempotency-key sched-001 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales emails get --email email_123 --contact contact_123 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales emails cancel --email email_123 --contact contact_123 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
```

- `draft`, `send` and `schedule` hit one route and set the body's `mode`. Body is plain text unless `--html`. `--cc` and `--bcc` repeat. Long bodies: `--data-file`.
- `--at` must be a future time with a timezone; a past or unreadable value exits 2 with no request.
- Create calls need `--idempotency-key` (API returns `400 idempotency_key_required`).
- A contact in an active sequence gets `409 active_sequence` unless `--allow-during-sequence`. Ask before using it: the person may get two emails.
- An email sent by an API key waits for Send Approval. Only a different key with `direct_email:approve` can approve it; the same key gets `403 self_approval_forbidden`.
- `cancel` works until the sender picks the email up; after that `409 already_sending`.

## Compose (new email to any address)

`firstsales emails compose --data-file compose.json` sends a new email to an address that may not be a contact yet. Body: `to`, `connectorId`, `subject`, `body`, `mode` (`draft`, `send` or `schedule`), plus the same optional fields as `emails send` and an optional `listId` to add the new contact to a list. It reaches a real person unless `mode` is `draft`: confirm first.
