# Deals, pipelines and activities

A pipeline is a row of stages. A deal sits in one stage and moves along. Activities are notes, calls and meetings logged against a contact.

```bash
firstsales pipelines list --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales deals list --stage stage_123 --q acme --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales deals get --deal deal_123 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales deals forecast --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales deals create --data '{"contactId":"ct_123","value":5000,"currency":"USD","pipelineId":"pl_123","ownerId":"user_123"}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales deals move --deal deal_123 --data '{"stageId":"stage_456"}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales activities log --data '{"type":"note","contactId":"ct_123","payload":{"text":"Asked for pricing"}}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
```

- `deals list` filters: `--stage`, `--pipeline`, `--owner`, `--q`, `--page`, `--limit`.
- `deals create` needs `contactId`, `value`, `currency`, `pipelineId`, `ownerId`. Optional: `companyId`, `stageId`, `expectedClose`, `labels`.
- `deals update` takes any of `contactId`, `companyId`, `value`, `currency`, `expectedClose`, `ownerId`, `labels`. Change the stage with `deals move`, not `update`.
- `activities log` needs `type` (`note`, `call`, `meeting`, `task` or `file`), `contactId` and `payload`. Optional: `companyId`, `dealId`.
- `activities list` filters: `--contact-id`, `--company-id`, `--type`, `--page`, `--limit`.
- Get `ownerId` from `firstsales members list`, and stage ids from `pipelines get --pipeline pl_123`.

## Deleting (confirm first)

`deals delete --deal deal_123 --confirm` removes the deal. Ask first.

## Edge cases

- A `stageId` from another pipeline is refused (400).
- A deal id from another workspace returns 404 (exit 4).
