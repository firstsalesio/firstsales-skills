# Signals (Beta)

A signal watches LinkedIn posts and collects the people who comment on or react to them. Those people become leads.

Each signal has one or more targets. A target is a `person`, a `company` or a `keyword`. All targets of one signal share one daily credit budget.

```bash
firstsales signals list --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales signals create --data '{"name":"Voice AI posts","targets":[{"kind":"keyword","value":"voice AI"}],"dailyCreditBudget":100}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales signals update --signal sig_123 --data '{"enabled":false}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales signals analytics --signal sig_123 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales signals leads --signal-id sig_123 --sort score --limit 50 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales signals export --signal-id sig_123 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
```

- `create` body: `name` (1 to 80 characters), `targets` (at least one `{kind, value}`), `dailyCreditBudget` (whole number, 1 to 5000).
- `update` takes any of `name`, `targets`, `dailyCreditBudget`, `enabled`.
- `leads` filters: `--signal-id`, `--q`, `--engagement comment|reaction`, `--target`, `--from`, `--to`, `--min-score` (0 to 100), `--sort`, `--offset`, `--before`, `--limit` (at most 100).
- `export` takes the same filters, without paging.

## Spending credits (confirm first)

- `create` starts the first scan right away, then scans again each day. `signals run --signal sig_123` starts one now. Both use credits. Tell the user the daily budget, then get a yes.
- To stop spending, run `update` with `{"enabled":false}`.
- `signals delete --signal sig_123 --confirm` removes the signal. Ask first.

## Edge cases

- 402 `payment-required`: Signals needs a paid plan. Tell the user to upgrade in the app.
- 402 `insufficient-credits`: the organization has no credits left. See billing-usage.md.
- 409 `signal-paused`: `run` on a signal that is turned off. Turn it on first.
- 400 `signal-limit`: the workspace already has 20 signals, the most it can hold. Delete one first.
- 400 `source-unavailable`: that target kind is not available yet.
- 404 `not-found`: wrong signal id, or the signal is in another workspace.
