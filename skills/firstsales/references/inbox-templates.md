# Inbox and reply templates

The inbox holds reply threads from campaigns and direct email.

```bash
firstsales inbox threads --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales inbox thread --thread thread_123 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales inbox read --thread thread_123 --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales inbox bulk-read --data '{"threadIds":["thread_1","thread_2"]}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales inbox snooze --thread thread_123 --data '{"until":"2026-11-01T09:00:00Z"}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales inbox assign --thread thread_123 --data '{"assigneeId":"user_123"}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
```

- `threads` filters: `--tab`, `--sender-connector-id`, `--campaign-id`, `--category`, `--from`, `--to`, `--sort`, `--page`, `--limit`.
- `--tab` values: `all` (default), `needs-approval`, `unread`, `needs-reply`, `replies`, `completed`, `active`, `bounced`, `failed`, `warmup`, `snoozed`.
- `bulk-read` takes 1 to 200 `threadIds` per call.
- `snooze` needs a future `until` with a timezone, at most a year out. `{"until":null}` unsnoozes.
- `assign` takes `assigneeId` (a member id from `firstsales members list`). `{"assigneeId":null}` unassigns.

## Replies and drafts (sending: confirm first)

- `inbox score` and `inbox ai-draft` ask for a score or a draft. They never send.
- `inbox draft-content` reads a pending draft.
- `inbox approve-draft --email email_123 --idempotency-key approve-001` sends that draft to a real person. Show the draft text and get a yes.
- `inbox reject-draft --email email_123` drops it.
- `inbox reply --thread thread_123 --data-file reply.json` sends a reply. Show the text first.

## Reply templates

```bash
firstsales inbox templates --org org_123 --workspace ws_123 --json
firstsales inbox template-create --data '{"name":"Pricing","subject":"Pricing","body":"Hi, here is our pricing ..."}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales inbox template-update --template tpl_123 --data '{"body":"..."}' --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
firstsales inbox template-delete --template tpl_123 --confirm --org org_123 --workspace ws_123
```

Template body: `name` and `body` required, `subject` optional.

## Edge cases

- Approving a draft that was already sent or rejected returns 409.
- A thread id from another workspace returns 404 (exit 4).
